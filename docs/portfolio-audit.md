# Ricreations Digital Marketing Portfolio & Career Brand Audit

Date: 12 August 2026  
Primary objective: win interviews and professional opportunities in digital marketing  
Audit standard: recruiter clarity, commercial proof, marketing depth, credibility, UX, accessibility, SEO and technical readiness

## Scope and evidence limits

Verified in this audit:

- Current local homepage (`index.html`) at desktop and mobile widths.
- Current reusable case-study page and all three records in `case-studies.js`.
- Azzar Steel & Fencing, Kredibility Finance and Zibuko Capital case-study content.
- The unpublished Azzar Electric Gate Motors working case-study document.
- Local HTML, CSS and JavaScript structure.
- Public benchmark examples and official guidance available on 12 August 2026.

Not supplied or not verifiable:

- A live public portfolio URL and production hosting configuration.
- CV.
- Exact target job titles.
- Target country/region.
- Private LinkedIn profile state, full Experience, About, certifications and recommendations.
- Full Behance project inventory behind any access restrictions.
- Ads Manager, GA4, GTM, CRM or sales-system exports supporting published claims.

Where evidence is missing, this report says so. It does not treat a claim as proven merely because it appears in portfolio copy.

## Executive verdict

The portfolio looks like a capable creative professional who is moving into digital marketing. It does not yet look like a fully proven digital marketer who happens to have exceptional creative range.

The visual identity is confident and memorable. The strategic case-study framework is promising. The published evidence architecture is the failure point: the homepage does not show the case studies, the strongest new project is trapped in a Word document, one published case study contains placeholders, and several quantitative claims lack dates, source screenshots, reporting periods or internally consistent calculations.

Current overall score: **44/100**  
Current benchmark: **Level 2 — Junior Professional**, with visible Level 3 potential.  
Current interview verdict: **borderline no for performance/growth roles; possible yes for hybrid creative/digital roles if the CV is strong.**

# Phase 7 — Turning design work into marketing evidence

Weak framing:

> I designed social media posts for the campaign.

Strong framing:

> The audience needed quick reassurance on durability, security and installation fit. I translated those objections into three creative angles, adapted each for feed and story placements, and used campaign response data to decide which angle should receive additional budget. The winning message reduced cost per qualified enquiry from X to Y over Z days.

Reusable transformation:

1. Business challenge — what commercial constraint existed?
2. Audience — who makes the decision and what blocks action?
3. Insight — what evidence informed the message?
4. Strategy — what choice followed from that insight?
5. Execution — what assets, tracking and channels did you own?
6. Measurement — what events and decision thresholds were used?
7. Result — what moved versus the baseline?
8. Learning — what would you repeat or change?

Do not retrofit strategy that did not exist. If the work was production-only, say so and show execution quality rather than claiming campaign ownership.

## Accessibility risks

- Hero headline is built from `div/span`, not a semantic `h1`.
- No visible `main` landmark.

- GSAP hero and scroll animation do not fully respect reduced-motion preference.
- Link labels are visually small on mobile.
- Embedded reviews cannot be fully audited or controlled from the host page.
- Keyboard focus visibility is not consistently defined.

Screenshot-only review cannot prove full WCAG conformance; keyboard, screen-reader, zoom and computed contrast testing remain required.
